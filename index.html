ABOUT THE WORK

Design that's built to be seen.

I design graphic content and direct short-form video — from concept and storyboard through to final edit. Recent work spans themed lookbook campaigns in Canva and cinematic reels edited in CapCut, several of which I also directed and appeared in.

The throughline across both is storytelling: strong visual concepts, a clear narrative, and content built to stop the scroll and hold attention long enough to land the message.
